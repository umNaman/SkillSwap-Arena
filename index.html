Philosophy

WHY ANONYMOUS PRACTICE?

Identity biases distort real skill assessment. When no one knows who you are, only your words matter.

01

Eliminate Social Bias

Your college name, appearance, and social status disappear. Peers rate your actual arguments and communication.

02

Reduce Anxiety

Speaking anonymously reduces the fear of judgment. You practice at your real performance level, not your anxiety level.

03

Honest Peer Feedback

When raters are anonymous too, feedback is genuine — not shaped by friendship, politics, or hesitation.

04

Focus on Growth

Your performance data compounds across sessions. Track real skill improvement, not just one day's impression.

"The best way to improve how you speak is to speak more — with people who'll tell you the truth."
